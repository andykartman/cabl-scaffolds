# CABL scaffolds

Portable, plain-markdown "scaffolds": the principle under one part of Drew Dixon's AI life system, written so **your** AI can build its own version around how you work. Paste a scaffold into your AI's instructions or a project file and say the set-up line at the top of it.

They are not a product and not my system. My system is built around my own head and will not fit yours. Each scaffold is the part that transfers.

| # | Scaffold | What it gives your AI |
|---|----------|-----------------------|
| 01 | [Harness the chaos](scaffolds/harness-the-chaos-scaffold.md) | Capture every idea without chasing every idea |
| 02 | [An old MacBook as a 24/7 AI server, lid closed](scaffolds/mbp-24-7-lid-closed-server.md) | Power profile, launchd pattern, and the four watchdogs real failures taught me |

**Feedback and improvements:** open an Issue, or a Pull Request against the scaffold file. Tell me what your AI did with it; that is the most useful feedback there is.

**Provenance:** every scaffold says how much of it is my words and what Claude did. Setup and failures are mine; the writing is co-produced and I edit before anything ships.
